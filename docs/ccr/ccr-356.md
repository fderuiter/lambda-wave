# Change Control Record

**CCR ID:** CCR-20260709-356
**Version:** PR-356

## Problem Description
The kinematics module used standard arithmetic which could lead to invalid physical states such as negative distances or numerical instability (NaN, Infinity) during rapid patient movements or signal interruptions.

## Proposed Change
Implemented a clinical safety math layer to the kinematics module.
- Introduced the `SafetyResult` type to wrap kinematic outputs.
- Added configurable `ClinicalBounds`.
- Enforced non-negative constraints for distance, time, and frequency.
- Intercepted zero or near-zero denominators to prevent division by zero.
- Clamped velocity and acceleration within documented clinical breathing bounds.

## Impact on Hazards
- H-SYS-012: Prevents standard arithmetic from causing invalid states by enforcing clinical limits and returning structured SafetyResult data, thereby mitigating kinematic calculation errors.
