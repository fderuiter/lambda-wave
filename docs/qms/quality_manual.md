# Lambda-Wave Quality Manual

## 1. Scope
This Quality Manual describes the Quality Management System (QMS) established by Lambda-Wave to meet the requirements of ISO 13485 and FDA 21 CFR Part 820.

## 2. Quality Policy
Lambda-Wave is committed to providing safe and effective medical software. Our Quality Policy is to maintain the highest standards of safety and compliance through continuous improvement and automated governance.

## 3. Organizational Structure
The QMS covers the entire software development lifecycle, ensuring clear responsibilities for all personnel involved in product realization.

## 4. QMS Processes
All processes required for the QMS are documented in associated Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

### Associated SOPs
- SOP-01: Document Control and Approval
- SOP-02: Supplier Management and SOUP Analysis
- SOP-03: Risk Management and Hazard Analysis
- SOP-04: Change Control and Engineering Review

## 5. Traceability and Governance
Automated pipelines ensure end-to-end traceability from Corporate Quality Policies to technical implementation (e.g., source code, test cases) and Change Control Records (CCRs).
